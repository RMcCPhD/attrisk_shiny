Trial conduct interface in action

The interface has the following features:

  • Box A (top-left) - Trial design features
  • Box B (top-right) - Participant baseline characteristics
  • Box C (bottom-left) - Summary statistics
  • Box D (bottom-right) - Attrition risk over time

Trial design features

Box A displays the inputs for trial design features. Most features have a default value set upon opening the interface, but these can be changed by selecting the other available options for a feature.

The available set of trial design inputs are:

Features Description Categories and limits
Duration Duration of trial follow-up in weeks 12 to 520 weeks
Number of arms Number of treatment arms 2 to 6 arms
Sample size Approximate sample size Small (<500), Medium (500 - 999), Large (>=1000)
Estimated sample size Fine-tuning of sample size Optional input, default is midpoint of category (e.g. 250 if small)
Age limits Upper and lower age limits Upper: 70yr, >70yrs; Lower: 18yrs, >18yrs
BMI limits Upper and lower BMI limits Upper: None, <45kg/m2; Lower: None, <=25kg/m2
eGFR limits Upper and lower estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) limits Upper: None, <=60 or >60ml/min/1.73m2; Lower: None, <=30 or >30ml/min/1.73m2
Phase Trial phase Phase 3 or 4
Number of primary outcomes (POMs) Number of primary outcomes 1 or >1
Comparators Comparator treatment groups Placebo, Active comparator or both
Blinding type Level of blinding Open label, Double, Triple or Quadruple
WHO regions involved World Health Organisation (WHO) regions involved Can select more than one of the following: African, the Americas, Eastern Mediterranean, Europe, South-east Asia, Western Pacific

Participant baseline characteristics

Box B displays the inputs for participant baseline characteristics:

Features Description Categories and limits
Age Age in years 18 to 100 years
Sex Is the participant female or male? Female, Male
Race Which racial group does the participant identify with? Asian, Black/African American, White, Other
Current smoker Does the participant currently smoke? No, Yes
Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) duration Number of years since the participant was diagnosed with T2DM 0.01 (<1 week) to 20 years
Glycosylated haemoglobin (HbA1c) Blood glucose levels (% haemoglobin bound with glucose) 6 to 10%
Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) Kidney function measured by blood creatinine levels (ml/min/1.73m2) 60 to 120ml/min/1.73m2
BMI Body mass index (kg/m2) 18 to 50kgm/2
HDL cholesterol High density lipoprotein cholesterol (mmol/L) 1 to 3mmol/L
Total cholesterol Low density lipoprotein cholesterol (mmol/L) 4 to 10mmol/L
Systolic blood pressure (BP) Systolic blood pressure (mmHg) 60 to 140mmHg
Number of comorbidities Number of comorbidities (excluding T2DM) 0 to 10
Number of concomitant medications Number of concomitant medications (excluding those related to T2DM) 0 to 10

Summary statistics

Box C displays summary statistics for attrition risk over time.

The following columns are generated in this summary table:

Columns Description
Follow-up time (weeks) Each week of follow-up
Participants at risk Number of participants at risk of attrition at a given week
Non-completers Number of non-completers at a given week
Attrition risk (%) The probability of attrition having occurred at a given week

Attrition risk over time

Box D illustrates risk of attrition over time for a participant with certain characteristics in a trial with an inputted set of design features. The timeframe reflects the duration inputted in Box A. The y-axis illustrates risk of attrition (%), and the x-axis illustrates follow-up time in weeks.
