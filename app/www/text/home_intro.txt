AttRisk is an interactive application that allows users to estimate and visualise risk of participant attrition at the design and conduct stages of randomised controlled trials. 

The application has been built on models fitted to data from a large number of randomised controlled trials for type 2 diabetes mellitus and cancer. The data included both trial-level summaries and participant-level information about baseline characteristics and trial completion.

For trial design, users can create a randomised controlled trial with a particular set of features (e.g. duration, phase, treatment type) and evaluate how attrition might occur over time in that trial.

For trial conduct, users can expand this investigation by evaluating how attrition might occur over time in a trial enrolling participants with a particular set of characteristics (e.g. age, sex, race/ethnicity).
